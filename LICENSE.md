# The Madras School License (MSL v1.0)

*Rooted in the 18th-century Chennai Monitorial System — the historical ancestor of open knowledge sharing.*

## The Social Contract
1. **Free to Learn:** All knowledge, code, and curricula in this repository are free to inspect, study, and run without tuition, paywalls, or gatekeepers.
2. **Free to Fork:** You are encouraged to adapt, translate, and improve upon this work for your own community or hardware constraints.
3. **Pass It Forward:** Just as senior students in Madras taught junior peers to scale literacy to the masses, if you gain value from this work, share what you learn and mentor the next builder.

---

*Knowledge belongs to humanity. No permission needed.*
