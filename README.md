# Madras School of AI

> **Democratizing AI mastery through peer-to-peer learning, open-weights models, and real-world problem solving.**

---

## Why "Madras School of AI"?

In the late 18th century, the **Madras School System** (observed in Egmore, Chennai, and rooted in Tamil Nadu's *Thinnai Pallikoodam* tradition) revolutionized public education. By empowering senior students ("monitors") to teach junior peers using simple, accessible tools—like writing in the sand—it broke the monopoly of expensive private tutoring and proved that structured knowledge could scale to the masses at near-zero cost.

Modern AI training mirrors that exact architecture:
- **Teacher–Student Distillation:** Large frontier models distill their capabilities into smaller, open-weights models that anyone can run locally.
- **Reinforcement & Feedback Loops:** Iterative evaluation refines reasoning and weeds out errors.
- **Open Knowledge Dissemination:** Just as open university lectures and open-source repositories tear down tuition walls, open-weights AI removes paywalls and API gatekeepers.

**Madras School of AI** brings this 230-year-old Chennai legacy into the AI era.

## Core Principles

1. **Zero Gatekeepers, Zero Tuition**  
   Knowledge should not be locked behind steep fees or proprietary walls. All curricula, codebases, and workshops here are open and free.
2. **Proof of Work Over Badges**  
   We reject commercial rote learning, empty marks, and vanity certifications. You don't graduate by collecting digital badges—you graduate by shipping working systems that solve real problems.
3. **Peer-to-Peer Scaling (The Monitor Model)**  
   Every learner who masters a concept mentors the next cohort. Teaching is the ultimate test of understanding.
4. **Frugal & Local-First AI**  
   Like learning arithmetic in the sand without expensive textbooks, we prioritize **open-weights models**, quantization, local inference, and low-compute engineering over burning massive cloud budgets.
5. **No Rote Mimicry**  
   Historical monitorial schools risked shallow memorization when monitors lacked deep oversight. We counter this through rigorous code reviews, first-principles debugging, and live demos.

## Learning Tracks

- **Track 1: Foundations & First Principles**  
  Linear algebra, neural network internals, transformers from scratch, and reading foundational papers—paired with open university lectures (MIT, CMU, Stanford).
- **Track 2: Frugal AI & Open-Weights Engineering**  
  Running, quantizing, and fine-tuning open models (Llama, Qwen, Mistral, DeepSeek) on consumer hardware and edge devices.
- **Track 3: Real-World Systems & Local Impact**  
  Building production RAG pipelines, autonomous agents, and Indic/Tamil NLP tools that solve tangible civic, educational, and small-business problems.
